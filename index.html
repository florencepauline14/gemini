How do I support my child's astronaut dreams?

Feed their specific interests early, build discipline through music, get comfortable in water, and keep them moving for 30+ minutes a day.

How do I get scholarships for college abroad?

Ask for free guidance (CAMP PH, Edukasyon.ph, EducationUSA). Look at MEXT, GKS and MOE, and exchanges like Global UGRAD, Erasmus+ and JENESYS.

How do I apply for Masters or PhD scholarships?

Join GradMAP and Pinoy Scientists for step-by-step help. Target Chevening, Fulbright, Erasmus Mundus, Australia Awards, DAAD and DOST-SEI.

Which space organizations and missions can I join?

SGAC, SEDS and SEDS-Philippines, the Mars Society. For analog missions, look at Asclepios, HI-SEAS, LunAres and CHASM-PH.
