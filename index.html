My Path to Space

Dreams have a method. Here is exactly how I got here.

Childhood

Grew up in the Philippines with a deep curiosity for science. [Image Placeholder]

2015 – 2019: The Foundation

Moved to San Francisco to earn dual Bachelor's degrees in Natural Sciences (Biochemistry) and Social Sciences at Minerva University. Graduated Magna cum Laude. [Video Placeholder]

2019 – 2022: Industry & Medicine

Worked across South Korea and the US as a Scientific Research Associate at Enolink, developing prediction models for hospitals.

2022 – 2024: Pivoting to Space

Earned my Master's at the Barcelona Institute of Science and Technology (Ranked #1 in class). Began space biology research with Yuri Gravity, designing experiments for the ISS.

2025: Analog Astronaut

Served as Executive & Medical Officer for the Monsaraz Mars Analog Mission (ASP-01 NOVARES) in Portugal.

2026 – Present: Building the Future

Strategy Lead at Aequa (neuromodulation), establishing the Cebu Microgravity Consortium, and preparing satellite payloads for ISRO.

Projects

Current missions and past milestones.

Current Missions

Aequa

Strategy and Business Development Lead, working on magnetoelectric nanoparticles for non-surgical neuromodulation.

Cebu Microgravity Consortium

Establishing a network to conduct microgravity research in the Philippines and organizing short-duration parabolic flights.

LUZINHA Satellite Payload

Coordinating a biological experiment payload scheduled to be launched by ISRO in October 2026.

SpaceGen Academy

Serving as Team Lead at the Space Generation Advisory Council to build global space education platforms.

Past Milestones

Barcelona Zero-G Challenge

Flown in a parabolic flight; tested shear-thickening fluid as a cushion for force absorption in microgravity.

Monsaraz Mars Analog Mission

Conducted plant experiments (growing microgreens in Martian regolith) and DNA analysis in Portugal.

ISS Space Biology

Led experimental design and project management for internal and customer biological experiments aboard the ISS.
